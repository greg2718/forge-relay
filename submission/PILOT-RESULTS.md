# Real NEAR pilot evidence and limits

One buyer-funded task was observed on November 11, 2026. The task requested a 300–500 word report on listing research agents or MCP servers in Smithery, Glama and a third active directory. Each section needed prerequisites, fees or an explicit unknown, submission steps and supporting official sources; at least three direct HTTPS URLs were required.

## What happened

| Stage | Observed result | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Funding | Operator funded a $0.50 task from an existing NEAR USD balance | Real marketplace hiring and an escrow hold; not provider payout |
| First delivery | Returned text repeated the brief and offered generic methodology; zero source URLs | A real artifact was retrieved, but research requirements were unmet |
| Correction | Operator requested the missing requirements in the marketplace | Human-directed feedback, not autonomous negotiation |
| Second delivery | 116 whitespace-delimited words including headings, an empty-brief marker, zero URLs and no directory findings | Correction did not satisfy the task |
| Verification | Automatic citation-presence check failed both versions; manual requirement review also failed both | The system detected this missing-evidence case; no general accuracy rate is established |
| Dispute | Operator opened a full $0.50 refund dispute; exact deposit hold $0.0250 | Escalation submitted; last confirmed status is under review, not refunded |
| Bench record | Private isolated evaluation records requirements, versions, checks and pending dispute | A review artifact exists; original FLOP Bench was not invoked, and Router exclusion is advisory only |

These hashes identify reviewed artifacts; they do not independently attest that marketplace events occurred. This is an operator-reported pilot summary. Private job links, full job messages, credentials, account balances and billing records are intentionally not public. A failed delivery from one task does not establish fraud or broad provider unreliability.

## What remains unproven

No successful externally researched report was accepted. No provider payout, refund, or dispute decision is confirmed. No independent buyer demand was converted into paid accepted work: the pilot buyer was the project operator. Cross-market proposals combine discovery evidence; execution and settlement were tested only with one NEAR provider. NEAR Intents, blockchain subaccounts, pooled buying, sealed bidding and cross-chain settlement are not implemented. The local extractor/writer/verifier workers are deterministic processes, not independent LLM agents.

## Next measurable experiment

After the owner separately approves any future pilot, compare a buyer's single-market shortlist with a cross-market shortlist for the same requirement. Record eligible requests, quotes, hires, accepted deliverables, time to acceptance, total cost and rework. Use accepted deliverables per eligible request as the conversion measure. A larger sample with independent buyers is required before claiming conversion or economic uplift. No additional paid experiment is part of this submission preparation.
