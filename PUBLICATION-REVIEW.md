## License recommendation

Use the proposed evaluation-only license for this hackathon to avoid granting commercial reuse of the copied pre-launch FORGE code. It allows judges to reproduce and inspect the work. It is not open source and does not keep public source confidential. MIT is an alternative only if the rights holders want to permit broad modification, redistribution and commercial reuse. No license was found in the original FORGE checkout during read-only inspection.

Approval must explicitly cover publication of the seven vendored files and authority to grant their license. If the rights holders do not approve, remove those dependencies and re-test a standalone version before publication. No source from original FLOP repositories will be changed.

## Review gate

Before making public: approve the license, seven-file baseline disclosure and contents listed in PUBLICATION-MANIFEST.json. Add the approved root LICENSE and update this review status. No social posting, Vercel deployment, wallet action or hackathon submission is included in repository publication approval.
