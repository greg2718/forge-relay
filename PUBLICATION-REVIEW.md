# FORGE Relay publication review

## Adopted license

The owner approved the evaluation-only license in `LICENSE` for this hackathon to avoid granting commercial reuse of the copied pre-launch FORGE code. It allows judges to reproduce and inspect the work. It is not open source and does not keep public source confidential. MIT is an alternative only if the rights holders want to permit broad modification, redistribution and commercial reuse. No license was found in the original FORGE checkout during read-only inspection.

Approval covers publication of the seven vendored files and authority to grant their evaluation license. No source in the original FLOP repositories was changed.

## Approval scope

The approved contents are listed in PUBLICATION-MANIFEST.json. The proposed license was adopted as the root LICENSE; implementation files remain unchanged. No social posting, Vercel deployment, wallet action or hackathon submission is included in repository publication approval.
